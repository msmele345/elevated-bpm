Guided lab

Layer by job, not by habit.

  1. 00:00–03:00Recall the kick pattern—1, 5, 9, 13—without looking. Run it near 132–138 BPM.
  2. 03:00–08:00Add Closed Hat on 3, 7, 11, 15. Solo it for one loop, then return it to the kick. Hear the down–up handoff.
  3. 08:00–12:00Add Clap on 5 and 13—beats 2 and 4. Mute it for one loop, then restore it.
  4. 12:00–15:00Place Open Hat on 16. Test it briefly on 15 under the closed hat, hear the choke, then move it back.
  5. 15:00–18:00Click lit Kick steps 1 and 9 once more to accent them. Remove the hats for two loops, then bring the lift back.
Listen for: four different jobs, not four unrelated sounds—floor, lift, reply, turnaround.

Hear this

Choke

A closed hat cuts off a ringing open hat. Moving the open hat from 15 to 16 gives it room to pull into the next bar.

Retrieval check

Say the roles.

Why are hats on 3, 7, 11, 15? Why are claps on 5 and 13?

Reveal answer

The hats fall halfway between kicks. Steps 5 and 13 begin beats 2 and 4, the common backbeat positions.
