# Lesson 4 — Shape an Acid Voice

**Time:** 17 minutes  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Use:** Elevated BPM’s BL-303 **Cutoff**, **Resonance**, and **Decay** knobs  
**Primary visual aid:** [Open the HTML lesson](04-shape-an-acid-voice.html)

## The win

By the end, you will be able to predict what each bass knob changes, build two contrasting patches, and perform a controlled filter movement while the groove runs.

Use the bassline from Lesson 3. Press **Play** and leave it running.

## 00:00–04:00 — Learn the three jobs

Change one control at a time across a wide range. Listen before reading the description.

| Control | Plain-language job | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cutoff** | Chooses how much high-frequency content passes through the low-pass filter | Low = dark/muffled; high = bright/buzzy |
| **Resonance** | Emphasizes frequencies around the cutoff point | Low = rounded; high = ringing/squelchy |
| **Decay** | Sets how long the bass amplitude falls away | Short = pluck and space; long = connected/rumbling |

Cutoff and resonance interact: resonance highlights the moving boundary set by cutoff. Decay changes time, so it changes how much notes overlap the spaces in your rhythm.

## 04:00–08:00 — Build Patch A: dark and tight

Use the knob readouts as targets, not commandments:

- **Cutoff:** 300–600 Hz
- **Resonance:** 3–7 Q
- **Decay:** 0.10–0.22 s

Listen for a compact bass that leaves room above it for hats and stabs. If the pitch is hard to hear, raise cutoff slightly rather than changing every control.

Write down your actual values:

- Cutoff: __________
- Resonance: __________
- Decay: __________

## 08:00–12:00 — Build Patch B: bright acid

Move one knob, listen for a loop, then move the next:

- **Cutoff:** 900–2,500 Hz
- **Resonance:** 11–16 Q
- **Decay:** 0.18–0.38 s

High resonance makes the cutoff region speak more clearly. Sweep cutoff slowly and listen for vowel-like movement. If the result gets piercing, lower resonance before assuming the whole patch is wrong.

Write down your actual values:

- Cutoff: __________
- Resonance: __________
- Decay: __________

## 12:00–15:00 — Perform one controlled sweep

1. Start from Patch A or another dark setting.
2. Count **four loops** of the one-bar pattern.
3. Loop 1: hold the settings still.
4. Loops 2–3: open **Cutoff** gradually.
5. Loop 4: close it back toward the start.

Keep Resonance and Decay still. The point is to connect one hand movement to one audible change.

## Retrieval check

**15:00–17:00 — Predict before touching**

For each prompt, say the answer first, then test it:

1. “I need more brightness without longer notes.”
2. “I want the filter edge to ring harder.”
3. “I want more silence between bass hits.”

- [ ] You can describe all three knobs without reading the table.

## Keep the idea

The next time you open the sandbox, predict a knob move in words before touching it: “darker,” “more ring,” or “shorter.” Prediction turns knob-twisting into sound design.
