Field guide 02 · Any level

Find your way around.

The instrument now comes first. Play, tempo and the drums are on screen from the first click; sharing, the path and the mix bus wait until you ask for them.

  • share menu
  • transport strip

The one idea

What you play stays on screen. Everything else waits.

The transport strip pins itself to the top of the screen, so you can start, stop and switch patterns from anywhere on the Deck. Share, the full lesson path and the Master strip have moved out of the way of the instrument.

Control map

The transport strip.

Directly under the header. Scroll down to the bass or the stab and it stays pinned to the top. One slim row on a desktop, two on a phone.

ControlWhat it does
Play / StopStarts and stops the loop. Its LED is lit while playing.
TempoThe fader. 60–200 BPM.
BPM readoutThe current tempo.
Swing50–75%. See page 01.
A B C DThe pattern bank’s four slots. See page 03.
Copy, ClearBank tools. See page 03.

Control map

The Share ▾ menu.

In the header, top right. Press Share ▾ to open it; Escape or a click outside closes it.

  • LINK OUT · Share beat — encodes your beat into a URL and copies it. If the browser blocks copying, the URL appears in a field to select. A link carries programming only, never uploaded audio.
  • BUNDLE · Export bundle — saves the whole project, sounds included. Only send audio you have the right to share.
  • BUNDLE · Open a bundle — loads a bundle someone sent you.
Opening someone’s beat: a full-width Shared beat preview strip appears under the header, never inside the menu. Keep this beat saves it over your project; Back to my project puts yours back exactly as it was. A damaged or incompatible link shows an error strip with Dismiss.

Control map

The Curriculum line and the lesson card.

One line holds the Techno, Acid and Sampling track buttons, each with its own count (Acid 2/7), a progress meter, the current lesson, and Path ▸, which opens the numbered path. Press any lesson to jump to it; nothing is locked. Path ▾ folds it away, and it folds itself away when you reload. Moving between lessons never touches your beat.

The card below it reads Lesson 3 of 7. ✕ puts it away and a Resume lesson · … chip brings it back. Show me, at the end of the intro, scrolls the control the lesson points at back into view.

When you enter a lesson — from the path, from Next ▸, or by resuming — the Deck scrolls its control into view if it is not already fully on screen. It never scrolls on its own while you work. Under reduced motion the scroll jumps instead of gliding.

Reading the card

Progress chips.

One chip per thing the lesson checks. A met goal turns green with a ✓, so a five-part capstone reads as a checklist. A chip never says which step is wrong.

ChipWhat it is telling you
Kick 3/4Three of the four steps the lesson wants are on.
Kick 4/4 · 1 extraAll four are on, plus one it does not want. Find the extra and turn it off.
Bass notes 5/8Five notes placed, eight needed.
Bass pitches 2/3Two different pitches, three needed.
Env Mod sweep 40%40% of the turn the lesson asks for. Keep turning while the loop plays.
Tempo 130 → 138–145You are at 130 BPM; the target is 138–145. Go up.
Swing 50% → 56–66%You are at 50%; the target is 56–66%.

A screen reader announces a chip only when it turns green, and the lesson when it completes.

Reading the card

Once a lesson is complete.

  1. The card celebrates: Locked in — goal complete. Keep playing as long as you like. The path waits for you.
  2. Then it shrinks to a slim strip: ✓ Lesson complete, the lesson’s name, Reopen and Next: … ▸. The Deck comes back on screen.
  3. Next: … ▸ moves to the next lesson you have not earned. When the track has nothing left to earn, it reads Done.
  4. Reopen shows the full text again; Collapse shrinks it back.

Control map

“Load a sound” cards.

A pad with no sound and no programmed steps shows as a compact Load a sound card: the pad’s number (its key, 1–4), Pick a source for any sound already on the Deck, From file to load a file straight onto that pad — and it takes a dropped file.

The pad’s full lane appears as soon as it has a sound. A pad keeps its lane if it has programmed steps, even if its audio has gone missing, and if it is muted or soloed.

Screen sizes

Wide and narrow.

1600 px or wider: two columns — Drum Machine and Sampler left, Bass Line and Chord Stab right; header, transport, Curriculum and lesson card across the top, Master across the bottom.

Under 600 px: each lane puts its name, M, S and Level on one line and wraps its 16 steps into two rows of eight. The bass Accent and Slide rows wrap with it. Nothing scrolls sideways, down to 320 px.

Retrieval check

Find it without looking.

You are down at the stab keyboard — how do you stop the loop? A chip reads Clap 2/2 · 1 extra — is the lesson done? You finished a lesson and want to keep jamming — what do you press?

Reveal answer

Stop in the transport strip, still pinned to the top. No — one extra step is on; turn it off. Nothing — the path waits until you press Next: … ▸.
