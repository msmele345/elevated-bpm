# Field Guide 02 — Find Your Way Around the Deck

**Covers:** the **Share ▾** menu, the sticky transport strip, the **Curriculum** line, progress chips, the completed-lesson strip, **Show me**, "Load a sound" cards, wide screens, phones, skip links  
**Level:** Any  
**Primary visual aid:** [Open the HTML page](02-find-your-way-around.html)

## The one idea

The instrument comes first. Everything you need to play is at the top of the screen and stays there; everything else waits until you ask for it.

## The Share ▾ menu

**Where:** the header, top right.

Press **Share ▾** to open it. Inside:

- **LINK OUT · Share beat** — encodes your beat into a URL and copies it to your clipboard. If the browser will not let the Deck copy, the URL appears in a field for you to select and copy yourself. A link carries programming only, never uploaded audio.
- **BUNDLE · Export bundle** — saves a file holding the whole project, sounds included. Only send audio you have the right to share.
- **BUNDLE · Open a bundle** — loads a bundle someone sent you.

Escape or a click anywhere outside closes the menu.

When you open someone's link or bundle, a full-width **Shared beat preview** strip appears under the header — never inside the menu, because it needs your answer. **Keep this beat** saves it over your project; **Back to my project** puts yours back exactly as it was. Your saved work is untouched until you choose. A damaged or incompatible link shows a full-width error strip with **Dismiss** instead.

## The transport strip

**Where:** directly under the header. When you scroll down to the bass or the stab, it stays pinned to the top of the screen.

Left to right:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play / Stop** | Starts and stops the loop. Its LED is lit while playing. |
| **Tempo** | The fader. 60–200 BPM. |
| **BPM** readout | The current tempo. |
| **Swing** | 50–75%. See [page 01](01-feel-swing-and-level.md). |
| **A B C D** | The pattern bank's four slots. See [page 03](03-the-pattern-bank.md). |
| **Copy**, **Clear** | Bank tools. See [page 03](03-the-pattern-bank.md). |

On a desktop it is one slim row. On a phone it takes two.

## The Curriculum line

**Where:** under the transport strip.

One line holds:

- **Track buttons** — **Techno**, **Acid** and **Sampling**, each with its own count, such as **Acid 2/7**. Press one to switch tracks. Nothing is locked: you can start any track at any time.
- **A progress meter** for the track you are on.
- **The current lesson** — its number and name.
- **Path ▸** — opens the numbered path of every lesson on that track. Press any lesson to jump to it. Press **Path ▾** to fold it away again. The path folds itself away when you reload.

Moving between lessons never touches your beat. Your programming, tempo and knobs stay as they are.

## The lesson card

**Where:** under the Curriculum line.

### While you are working on it

- The top line says **Lesson 3 of 7** (or wherever you are). **✕** puts the lesson away; a **Resume lesson · …** chip brings it back.
- The intro text tells you what to do.
- **Show me** — at the end of the intro, when the lesson points at a control. Press it to scroll that control into view. Use it after you have scrolled away and lost track of the Env Mod knob or the keyboard.
- **Progress chips** — one per thing the lesson checks, in a row under the intro.

### Reading progress chips

Each chip counts toward one goal. When that goal is met, the chip turns green with a **✓**. A capstone that checks five things shows five chips, so it reads as a checklist.

| Chip | What it is telling you |
|---|---|
| **Kick 3/4** | Three of the four steps the lesson wants are on. |
| **Kick 4/4 · 1 extra** | All four are on, plus one step it does not want. Find the extra and turn it off. The chip never says which step it is. |
| **Bass notes 5/8** | Five notes placed, eight needed. |
| **Bass pitches 2/3** | Two different pitches, three needed. |
| **Env Mod sweep 40%** | You have turned the knob 40% of the distance the lesson asks for. Keep turning while the loop plays. |
| **Tempo 130 → 138–145** | You are at 130 BPM. The target is 138–145. Go up. |
| **Swing 50% → 56–66%** | You are at 50%. The target is 56–66%. |

A screen reader announces a chip only when it turns green, and announces the lesson when it completes — not every count along the way.

### Once it is complete

The card celebrates first: **Locked in — goal complete.** Keep playing as long as you like. The path waits for you.

After the celebration, the card shrinks to a slim strip: **✓ Lesson complete**, the lesson's name, **Reopen** and **Next: … ▸**. The Deck comes back on screen.

- **Next: … ▸** moves to the next lesson you have not earned. When the track has nothing left to earn, it reads **Done**.
- **Reopen** shows the lesson's full text again. **Collapse** shrinks it back.

### Scrolling to the control

When you enter a lesson — from the path, from **Next ▸**, or by resuming it — the Deck scrolls the control it points at into view, if that control is not already fully on screen. It does this only when you arrive. It never scrolls on its own while you are working, so the page never moves under a knob you are dragging. With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, the scroll jumps instead of gliding.

## The Sampler's "Load a sound" cards

A pad with no sound and no programmed steps shows as a compact **Load a sound** card instead of a row of silent steps. Each card has:

- the pad's number (its computer key, **1**–**4**);
- **Pick a source** — choose from any sound already loaded on the Deck;
- **From file** — load an audio file straight onto that pad;
- and it takes a dropped audio file.

The pad's full lane appears as soon as it has a sound. A pad keeps its lane if it has programmed steps, even if its audio has gone missing, and if it is muted or soloed, so you never lose sight of work you have done.

## Wide screens and phones

- **1600 px wide or more:** the Deck splits into two columns. Drum Machine and Sampler on the left, Bass Line and Chord Stab on the right. The header, transport strip, Curriculum and lesson card span the top; Master spans the bottom.
- **Under 600 px wide:** each lane puts its name, **M**, **S** and Level on one line and wraps its 16 steps into two rows of eight — steps 1–8, then 9–16 — keeping the four-step colours. The bass Accent and Slide rows wrap the same way, so step 5's accent sits under step 5's note. Nothing scrolls sideways, down to a 320 px screen.

## Skip links for keyboard users

Press **Tab** once on a fresh page and the skip links appear, in the Deck's order: **Skip to transport**, **Skip to curriculum**, **Skip to drum machine**, **Skip to sampler**, **Skip to bass synth**, **Skip to stab synth**, **Skip to master**. Press **Enter** on one and the next **Tab** lands inside that section.

## Retrieval check

1. You scrolled down to the stab keyboard. How do you stop the loop?
2. A chip reads **Clap 2/2 · 1 extra**. Is the lesson done?
3. You finished a lesson and want to keep jamming before moving on. What do you press?
