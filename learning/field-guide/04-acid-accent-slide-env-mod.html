Bend the bassline.

The BL-303 now has the flags and the knob that make acid: accents that bite, slides that glide, and an Env Mod knob that opens the filter on every note.

The one idea

One busy line that bends.

Accents make some notes bite. Slides make some notes glide into the next. Env Mod makes every note open the filter and close it again. Twelve notes on three pitches, three accents, two slides:

Control map

Everything is in the Bass Line panel.

  1. The Bass note row — click a step to place a note; ↑/↓ moves it a semitone, Shift+↑/↓ an octave, ←/→ sets its length.
  2. The Accent row — 16 thin toggles directly under the notes.
  3. The Slide row — 16 thin toggles under that.
  4. The knobs — Cutoff, Resonance, Decay and now Env Mod.

Each toggle sits under the note it belongs to — on a phone too, where all three rows wrap into two rows of eight together.

Control behavior

Accent, Slide, Env Mod.

Accent. The note plays about 4 dB louder — the same gap as a drum accent — and throws the filter open further and faster, so it barks while the rest purrs. It works even with Env Mod at 0%. Accents off the beat sound most like acid.

Slide. The note holds its gate open into the next step. If that step has a note, the line glides into it over about 60 ms without starting it again, and a tie mark on the Bass row joins the two. If that step is empty, the note holds through it and then stops. On a step with no note, the slide waits; place a note there later and it comes into play.

Env Mod. The fourth knob, 0–100%, default 0%. It sets how far every note opens the filter before Decay closes it — the “wow” at the front of each note. At 0% the bass sounds as it did before v3; at 100% each note throws the filter about four octaves above Cutoff. Cutoff still sets where each sweep starts, and Resonance how hard the moving edge rings.

Flags on empty steps: Accent and Slide stay set with no note under them, and turning a note off and on again keeps them. A screen reader says so: “Bass slide, step 5, on (no note)”.

Guided lab

Build the line above.

  1. Press Play.
  2. On the Bass row, click steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15 and 16. Each new note is C2.
  3. Focus step 4 and press Shift+↑ to make it C3. Do the same on step 12.
  4. Focus step 6 and press ↑ three times for E♭2. Do the same on step 15.
  5. On the Accent row, switch on steps 4, 10 and 12.
  6. On the Slide row, switch on steps 6 and 12. Two ties appear on the Bass row.
  7. Set Env Mod to about 50% and Resonance to about 12 Q.
  8. Set Swing to about 58%.
  9. Ride Cutoff and Env Mod while it plays.
Listen for: E♭2 bending down into C2 on steps 6–7 and C3 bending down on steps 12–13, with the accented notes cutting through.

Vocabulary

Tie.

The mark on the Bass row that joins a slid note to the note it glides into. No tie means the slide leads into a rest — it holds the note, but it does not count for Slide Into It.

Keyboard

A whole line, no mouse.

The Accent and Slide toggles are ordinary buttons: Tab to one, Enter or Space to flip it. On Env Mod, arrows nudge, Page Up/Page Down jump, Home is 0% and End is 100%.

Retrieval check

Predict, then test.

A slide on step 8, and step 9 is empty — what happens? Env Mod at 0% — does an accent still sound different? Bass slides 1/2, but two slides are on — why?

Reveal answer

Step 8’s note holds through step 9 and stops. Yes — an accent always adds level and its own filter kick. One slide leads into an empty step; only slides into a following note count.
