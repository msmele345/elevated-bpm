Know the new deck.

Where every v3 control is and what it does. The First Groove course teaches you to make techno; this guide tells you what the new buttons and knobs are for, one page per strand.

The one idea

Nothing changes until you touch it.

Every new setting starts at the sound the Deck already made: swing 50%, every Level at 0.0 dB, Env Mod 0%, no accents or slides, slots B–D empty. Read the page for the control you are about to use, then try it while the loop plays.

Control map

Where everything sits now.

  1. Header — the Elevated BPM name and the Share ▾ menu
  2. A shared-beat strip or a link error, only when one needs your answer
  3. Transport strip — Play, Tempo, BPM, Swing, slots A–D, Copy, Clear. It stays pinned to the top of the screen.
  4. Curriculum line — track buttons, progress, the current lesson, Path ▸
  5. The lesson card
  6. Drum Machine
  7. Sampler
  8. Bass Line
  9. Chord Stab
  10. Master — scope, Filter and Drive macros, FX
First click: on a fresh load at 1280×720, Play, the tempo and all five drum lanes are on screen before you scroll.

How to use it

Out of order is fine.

This is a reference, not a course. Open the page for the control in front of you. For guided practice, start the First Groove course.

Retrieval check

Before you open a page.

Where would you look for the Swing knob? Where would you look for the bass’s mute button?

Reveal answer

Swing is in the transport strip, beside the BPM readout. The bass’s M, S and Level sit in the Bass Line panel header, at the same end as a drum lane’s.
